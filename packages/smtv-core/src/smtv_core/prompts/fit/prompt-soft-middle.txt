In a multi-voice game or call, other voices often drop short lines that belong to the game or to someone else (a call-out, a command, a question to another player, a remark about the map). Such a short interjection inside the interval does not break the episode when the main line resumes right after it on the same premise, question, action or outcome: keep it inside the trim and judge the main line. It is a break only when the main line itself stops and the following lines no longer continue its premise.
