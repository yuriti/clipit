# The StreamMoment account

> 🕓 **Coming soon.** Sign-in to a StreamMoment account is not open yet: the **Sign in** button in SMTV says so. SMTV
> works fully without an account — below is what to do right now.

## What the account will give you

Two things will work through the account:

- **Links to Twitch and YouTube.** The recording will be downloaded through our server — fast and with no setup:
  paste a link, and that's it. The server passes the recording to SMTV in pieces and stores nothing. This is the next
  update.
- **A subscription.** Pay once a month, and SMTV uses OpenAI's AI by itself: no keys or services to deal with. It
  will be enough for about 50 streams, or about 90 hours of recordings a month (the numbers are preliminary).

You will sign in with **Google**, **Twitch** or **Telegram** in **Settings → Account**. More about the plans: the
[«What's next»](../../../README.en.md#whats-next) section.

## What to do now

**An AI.** Choose one in **Settings → AI**:

- [your own key](get-a-key.md) from an AI service — for example OpenAI, or the cheaper DeepSeek; you pay the service
  directly, only for what you use;
- [a free AI on your computer](free-ai-on-your-computer.md) — Qwen through Ollama or LM Studio; it needs a strong
  graphics card.

Not sure which? [Which AI to choose](connect-ai.md).

**The stream recording.**

- Download the recording yourself and pick the file — that always works.
- Or turn on **Download links myself** in **Settings → VodCut**: the yt-dlp program downloads the link on your
  computer (**Settings → Computer check** installs it). It does not always work: YouTube may ask you to sign in.

More in [Your first clip](first-clip.md).

## What the account will see

- Your clips and video files stay on your computer.
- For a link, StreamMoment's server takes the recording from the platform and passes it to SMTV in pieces; it stores
  nothing.
- With the subscription, the text of the stream goes through the account to the AI, to choose the moments.
